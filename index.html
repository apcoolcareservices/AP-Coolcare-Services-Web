Ask a question
How often should my aircon be cleaned?

For homes, a general cleaning every 3 to 6 months keeps units efficient. Offices and commercial spaces that run all day usually need it more often, which is what a preventive maintenance plan covers.

What's the difference between general cleaning, chemical cleaning and a chemical overhaul?

General cleaning is routine upkeep of filters, coils and drain. Chemical cleaning uses coil cleaners to remove mold and stubborn buildup when cooling has weakened. A chemical overhaul takes the unit apart for a full soak and wash, for units that are heavily clogged or barely cooling.

Which brands do you sell and service?

We are an authorized dealer partner of Koppel, LG, Everest, Carrier, Condura and Midea, and we service most other brands too. Tell us your unit's brand and model when you book.

Do you handle commercial and industrial sites?

Yes. We do site assessments, supply and installation, and preventive maintenance contracts for offices, buildings and factories, from a few units to dozens on one schedule.

Is there a warranty on new units?

New units carry the manufacturer's warranty. For example, LG backs its residential inverter models with a 10-year compressor warranty. Ask us about the warranty for the specific model you are considering.
